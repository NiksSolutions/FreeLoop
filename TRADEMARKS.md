# Trademarks

Freeloop names other companies' and projects' products to say what it works with: the model providers, the coding agents it starts, the systems it runs on and the themes its styles follow. Those names are their owners' trademarks. Freeloop uses them only to refer to those products, and is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by any of their owners.

## The name Freeloop

Freeloop is this project's name; the project claims no trademark in it. FREELOOP is registered by others for batteries and consumer electronics in the US, the EU and Japan, and FreeLoop by Huawei in Korea and Japan; none of these covers software. `docs/RISKS.md` (L-03) records the search.

## Logos

The marks that say what a session runs on show a vendor's logo only where the vendor's published terms let a third-party app show it. Freeloop ships three, each the vendor's file, unchanged; `frontend/src/logos/README.md` names each file's source and terms.

- **llama.cpp**: the icon from [ggml-org/llama-brand](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama-brand), under CC BY-NC 4.0 and ggml-org's grant in its `BRAND-USAGE.md` to reproduce and redistribute the marks, commercially included, to identify or refer to llama.cpp. The logo is from the ggml-org/llama-brand repository.
- **OpenRouter**: the glyph from [openrouter.ai/brand](https://openrouter.ai/brand).
- **vLLM**: the compact logo from [vllm-project/media-kit](https://github.com/vllm-project/media-kit).

Freeloop ships no other vendor's logo. Anthropic, OpenAI and Google ask for permission before their logos are used, and other vendors publish no terms that cover this use, so their marks show two letters. A user can give any mark a picture of their own, or click **Official logo** in Settings, which fetches the vendor's logo from the vendor's own website at that moment and keeps it on that user's computer; Freeloop does not distribute it. Checked 2026-11-27; `make check-logos` fetches each of those logos again and re-reads the terms behind these choices.

If you speak for one of these owners and want something here changed, please open an issue at [github.com/NiksSolutions/FreeLoop](https://github.com/NiksSolutions/FreeLoop/issues).
